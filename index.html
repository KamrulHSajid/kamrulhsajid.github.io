About Me

Hi, I'm Sajid. I've completed my Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2024. From a clueless freshman to a graduate achieving continuous academic excellence, my bachelor journey shows consistent growth. For my undergraduate thesis, I worked on the integration of the ever-changing language of the workplace with urban context and local people — a project that reflects my interest in a human interaction-centric design approach.

After graduation, I stepped into design consultancies to put my knowledge into practice, and became an Associate Member (AH-578) of the Institute of Architects, Bangladesh (IAB). I aspire to blend design acumen and architectural knowledge into the ever-evolving field of Human-Centered AI research.

My research interests are interdisciplinary, spanning Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), the Future of Work, Urban Informatics, Human-Centered AI, Smart Environments & Experiences, Data Visualization & Interaction, Design Computing, and the intersection of the Built Environment and HCI.

Publications

THESIS

Integration of Workplace with Urban Fabric and Local Inhabitants

Undergraduate Thesis — Supervisor: Dr. S M Najmul Imam

The study explored the integration of the ever-changing language of the workplace with urban context and local people. A business hub of the 21st century demands to perform not necessarily as a daytime workspace only — it can be an inclusive part of urban city life. A business hub can be re-introduced as a city square, a center for youth software enthusiasts, freelancers, and startup communities, and imagined as a gathering place for youth & local people discussing contemporary issues.

BOOK

Read A Building

Department of Architecture, BUET

"Read A Building" is a compilation of research work done by students of Batch '17, Department of Architecture, BUET, under the guidance of Studio Tutors, depicting the architectural features and character of two iconic buildings of Bangladesh — Fine Arts Institute, Dhaka University, and the Department of Architecture, BUET. It offers logical explanations and interpretations of the language of architecture, presenting an accessible guide to design elements, architectural styles, and the contexts in which they emerged.
