I've completed my Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) in 2024. From a clueless freshman to a graduate achieving continuous academic excellence, my bachelor journey shows consistent growth.

After graduation, I stepped into design consultancies to put my knowledge into practice, and became an Associate Member (AH-578) of the Institute of Architects, Bangladesh (IAB). I aspire to blend design acumen and architectural knowledge into the ever-evolving field of Human-Centered AI research.
